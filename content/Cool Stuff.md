# Websites and Tools

>[!tip] The [[Hardware Software#Applications|Applications]] section over at [[Hardware Software|🖥️Hardware and Software]] has some applications that run in browser!
#### 🌟 [Topsters](https://topsters.org/)
Create those nice looking favorite album charts.
	(or games, books, movies, shows, anything really)
#### [OpenTierBoy](https://www.opentierboy.com/rank)
Custom Tier List creator. Open source.
#### 🌟 [Lichess](https://lichess.org/)
Free (mostly) open-source Chess engine.
	A lot of people even prefer it to chess.com, despite that being paid.
#### [KidPix](https://kidpix.app/)
A port of KidPix to browser.
#### 🌟 [MonkeyType](https://monkeytype.com/)
Test keyboards and your WPM.
	- [**Typeracer**](https://play.typeracer.com/):
		If you prefer to race passages against others in real time, then check out Typeracer!
#### 🌟 [Catbox.moe](https://catbox.moe/)
A nice, free, minimum bullshit image and file cloud.
	Also check out [Litterbox](https://litterbox.catbox.moe/) for hosting files which are temporary in nature and can just be deleted whenever.
#### 🌟 [Filmot](https://filmot.com/)
Search for YouTube videos by their transcripts.
	OP for trying to find that one moment or video you have no idea how to begin searching for.
	As a tip: Replace any swears with `#FUCK#`. This is regardless of the word, its always `#FUCK#` specifically, even if it's actually "shit", for example. This is due to transcript censorship on YouTube's end.
	- [**NLQuotes**](https://nlquotes.com/)
		Search transcripts of Northernlion vids and VoDs. Supported by [The Librarian](https://www.youtube.com/c/TheLibraryofLetourneau).
#### [MyTime.io](https://mytime.io/)
Converts to your local timezone as based on the url.
	You can format the url as something like mytime.io/13:00/PT or mytime.io/1pm/PT to have 1pm PT converted into your timezone automatically. It'll give the correct time for anyone connecting. Be sure to turn off your VPN.
#### [When2Meet](https://www.when2meet.com/)
Geared towards scheduling events with others, see when your free time overlaps.
#### [ASCII Art Archive](https://www.asciiart.eu/)
Nice little ASCII site. Has my favorite ASCII generators, drawing tools, and more.
#### [Neal.fun](https://neal.fun/)
Cool little distractions by [a guy named Neal](https://twitter.com/nealagarwal).
#### [Spellcheck, a word game](https://spellcheck.xyz/)
Spelling bee browser game, its neat
#### [Bingosync](https://bingosync.com/)
Lets you create collaborative bingo-boards with others.
	The primary use case for this is videogame challenges, particularly using the lockout mode. Though I recommend [this mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/yet-another-minecraft-bingo) if you want to do this with Minecraft.
#### [Fur Finder](https://sproutsberry.ca/projects/fur-finder/)
Online tool to find fur fabrics by arbitrary color.
	Automatically gives a number of close matches for a given selected color anywhere on the color spectrum from a number of faux fur vendors for fursuits, or anything else that you'd use fur fabric on. Can even use particular hex codes.
	Lab matching, advanced filters, and color entry.
	Note that hex probably isn't going to translate directly into real materials well, since hex is mostly a digital thing. Regardless, great tool for finding specific colors.
#### [Keygenmusic](https://keygenmusic.tk/)
Website that hosts a ton of music used in various keygen programs. Very fun!
#### [PetPet](https://benisland.neocities.org/petpet/)
Tool for creating those petting gifs memes.
#### [Quartz](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/)
What i used to make this site!
	Its basically tool that generates static-sites from markdown text. If you want a front-end for writing markdown text in, check out Obsidian.md in [[Hardware Software|🖥️Hardware and Software]]
# 24/7 Programming
#### [OldTimeyComputerShow](https://www.twitch.tv/oldtimeycomputershow)
Curated showcase of video tapes and films about computers and video games, from the 20th century.
	Covers history, promotional material, competitions, tutorials, computer art, and other ephemera.
#### 🌟 [SaltyBet](https://www.saltybet.com/)
Lets you place bets on live competitive events, though is usually running randomized CPUvCPU matches in MUGEN.
	During MUGEN, characters are moved into brackets that roughly reflect their strength, so its never clear exactly who will win. You can only gamble fictional currency associated with saltybet, so its all just for fun :)
#### [TwitchPlaysPokemon](https://www.twitch.tv/twitchplayspokemon)
Stream that plays Pokemon games, but Twitch chat is controlling the inputs.
	Always running something. Sometimes its a mainline game, sometimes its chat playing 2p battle revolution with bets, sometimes romhacks. Nothing will be as iconic as the first TPP, but its still a cool novelty nonetheless.
#### 🌟 [RadioGarden](https://radio.garden/)
Explore live radio from around the world online.
	I recommend checking out their tabs in the sidebar, they highlight some interesting stuff. 
	- UK stations restrict access: [unblock](https://www.reddit.com/r/RadioGarden/comments/18e12gf/a_way_to_get_past_the_radiogarden_restrictions/), [2](https://www.reddit.com/r/RadioGarden/comments/13b53zs/ive_found_a_way_of_bypassing_the_ukonly/), or use a [[Privacy Security#VPNs|VPN]]
	- **Highlights I Like**:
		- [datafruits.fm](https://datafruits.fm/)
			In Seattle. a cooperatively owned and operated free-form net radio station reated by and for fans of internet radio and netlabels, with little to no curation. crowdfunded and ad-free. Their website is also pawsome
		- [aNONradio.net](https://anonradio.net/)
			In Seattle. Literally "a non radio", since they're a non-commercial, non-profit net 'radio' station. Note that the station is an open-mic for all members of the [SDF Public Access UNIX System Community](https://sdf.org/?welcome)
		- [vintageobscura.net](https://vintageobscura.net/)
			In New York. Highlights "forgotten" music. Everything featured has less than 30k views on youtube at the time of discovery and were released before 96'

#### [Radio Plus Anime Super Go](http://animego.ddns.net:1010/anime.aac)
"Rare Anime and Science Fiction, Sounds, Soundtracks, Filk music, comedy, and more, streaming from my private collection, done in the style of a VIRTUAL RADIO STATION!"
	Radio station ran by [Jennifer Diane Reitz](https://jenniverse.com/), whom you may know if you're into outsider artists for being the writer of Pastel Defender Heliotrope, Unicorn Jelly, or her prolific works in The Conversion Bureau, as well as the designer of Boppin' for the amiga. its basically just a collection of stuff she likes. Silly, autistic, fun. That's the best way i can explain it. Makes me nostalgic randomly and unexpectedly.
# Health and Wellness
### Transgender
It is **never** too late.
#### [Transfeminine Science](https://transfemscience.org/)
Site dedicated to sharing information relating to gender-affirming transfeminine hormone (replacement) therapy (HRT).
	The articles on this site are wiki-esque living documents, and hence may be updated, expanded, and improved over time. It should be noted that not anyone can edit or add information to the site.
####  [diyhrt.info](https://diyhrt.info/)
Do It Yourself Hormone Replacement Therapy information. Both feminizing and masculinizing hormone replacements are covered.
	Especially useful if the system is failing you (underdosing, gatekeeping, waitlisting, potentially even banning, if SCOTUS decides to be particularly evil soon), but even barring that, it's an alternative to be considered to give you more control over your transition and possibly even save you some dosh. Also just a good resource for learning about hrt and it's effects in general (which you really ought to do. Most doctors do not have the time or need to research the ins and outs of transition, and are... safe but surprisingly very ignorant).
	- [old site](https://diyhrt.wiki)
#### 🌟 [hrt.cafe](https://hrtcafe.net/)
A repo of reputable distributors to buy DIY HRT from.
	Both feminizing and masculinizing hormone replacements are covered.
	If they're not reputable, they don't get on the list. Yes, its safe.

# Newsletters, RSS, and Podcasts
#### [FMHY RSS Tools](https://fmhy.net/internet-tools#rss-tools)
FMHY's list of RSS tools
	includes general tools, readers, and feed generators, if you want to into RSS.
	- [Kill the Newsletter](https://kill-the-newsletter.com/) - Tool that converts email newsletters into Atom RSS feeds
#### [Read Only Memo](https://readonlymemo.com/)
Bi-Weekly newsletter covering emulation and related topics
	Covers FPGA & MiSTer breakthroughs, big emu releases, game hacks and fan translations, new retro hardware, and any other big stories about the retro gaming scene.
	Their website also has an archive of all newsletters printed to date, afaik.
	- their [About page](https://readonlymemo.com/about/) has a longer pitch
#### [Tom Scott's Weekly Newsletter](https://www.tomscott.com/newsletter/)
Semi-retired travel blogging YouTuber [Tom Scott](https://www.youtube.com/@TomScottGo) curates stuff he likes
	He basically just compiles stuff he enjoys from YouTube, various articles, and whatever else. If you think you'd like Tom's taste, I think this newsletter is nice to have. Unshackle yourself from The Algorithm, or whatever. Personal curation is a beast.
	Sometimes unsubscribes me, which is annoying.
#### [Undertale / Deltarune Newsletter](https://toby.fangamer.com/newsletters/)
Where Toby Fox posts updates to UT/DR
	If you subscribe at the Super or higher tier, you'll also get to see basically anything Toby works on, which is very nice. Extremely rarely the newsletter will send out cryptic and secret newsletters to only a fraction of subscribers, so I recommend you subscribe to roll the dice at that if you're into Toby's work :)
#### [Nicky Case's Newsletter](https://ncase.me/sub/)
[Nicky Case](https://ncase.me/) is an extremely talented individual making cool stuff online
	This newsletter is extremely infrequent, but regardless Nicky makes a ton of important and great stuff. I recommend exploring her site and blog, you may recognize some of her work.
	- [Archive](https://ncase.me/archive/) doesn't have everything, [Blog](https://blog.ncase.me/) has more
	- She also serves an [RSS](https://ncase.me/rss/) feed, and this is a great writeup on the advantages of RSS as well.
#### [Survelliance Report](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3KeV6Ui_4CZemCIsHUIuEtugAmUxAYHS)
Privacy and Security news podcast hosted by [Techlore](https://www.youtube.com/@techlore).
	I use this for weekly privacy/security news, I often skim / skip to stories which are relevant to me and others I know.
	- [Techlore's site](https://techlore.tech/) and newsletter
#### [Privacy Guides](https://www.privacyguides.org/)
Privacy Guides is the go-to place for most everything privacy.
	They have a newsletter, a weekly livestream, data breach roundups, articles, critical messages/PSAs, etc. All of these can be opted in or out of.
	You can subscribe near the bottom of the page under "Stay connected"
